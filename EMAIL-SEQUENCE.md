## Email Best Practices

1. **Subject lines:**
   - Keep under 50 characters
   - Use curiosity ("The hidden cost...")
   - Avoid spam words (FREE!!!, CLICK NOW!!!)

2. **Body copy:**
   - Write like you're texting a friend
   - Use "you" and "I" (personal)
   - Break up long paragraphs
   - One CTA per email

3. **Timing:**
   - Send 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM Nairobi time
   - Tuesday-Thursday perform best
   - Avoid Mondays and Fridays

4. **Tracking:**
   - Monitor open rates (aim for 20%+)
   - Click rates (aim for 3%+)
   - Unsubscribe rate (keep under 0.5%)

## Personalization Variables

Use these in your email tool:

- `{{first_name}}` → "Hi David" instead of "Hi there"
- `{{signup_date}}` → "You signed up 3 days ago"
- `{{city}}` → "Fellow Nairobi agent" (if you collected this)

Personalized emails get 2-3X better open rates.
