- Never spam the same message in multiple groups

## Group Admin Tips

**If you're posting in groups you don't admin:**

1. **Read the rules first** - Some ban promotional posts
2. **Provide value first** - Post 3 helpful things before promoting
3. **Don't be salesy** - Share results, not features
4. **Engage genuinely** - Comment on other posts, help people

**If admins delete your post:**

- Don't repost immediately
- DM the admin: "Hey, I shared [topic]. Is there a better way to share value with the group?"
- Wait 1 week before trying again
- Focus on other groups

## Success Metrics

Track these per post:

- **Reach:** How many people saw it?
- **Engagement:** Comments + reactions
- **DMs:** How many people messaged you?
- **Signups:** How many clicked your link?

**Good benchmarks:**
- Reach: 100-500 people per post
- Engagement: 10-30 reactions, 5-15 comments
- DMs: 3-10 per promotional post
- Conversion: 10% of engaged people → signups

## What NOT to Do

❌ **Don't:** Post the same thing in 5 groups at once  
✅ **Do:** Customize each post slightly

❌ **Don't:** Be pushy in comments  
✅ **Do:** Be helpful and genuine

❌ **Don't:** Ignore comments  
✅ **Do:** Reply to EVERY comment within 2 hours

❌ **Don't:** Only post when selling  
✅ **Do:** Post value content 80% of the time

❌ **Don't:** Argue with skeptics  
✅ **Do:** Answer questions, ignore trolls
