# Case Study Template & Guide

How to capture and share customer wins that drive more signups.

---

## Why Case Studies Matter

**Social proof > Your claims**

"Close 30% more deals" = Marketing speak  
"David closed 3 extra deals in month one" = Proof

**Good case studies:**
- Build trust
- Show real results
- Overcome objections
- Drive signups

**One strong case study = 10 testimonials**

---

## When to Create Case Studies

**Timing:**
- After customer uses product for 30+ days
- After they've seen measurable results
- Before they churn (capture wins while fresh)

**Ideal candidates:**
- Used product consistently (daily/weekly)
- Closed at least 1 extra deal
- Willing to share name/photo
- Good communicator

---

## Case Study Interview Questions

**Send these via WhatsApp before the call:**

### Part 1: Before LeadFlow

1. What was your biggest challenge managing WhatsApp leads before LeadFlow?
2. How many leads were you losing track of per week?
3. What tools or systems did you try?
4. What wasn't working about them?
5. How much time were you spending organizing WhatsApp daily?

### Part 2: Using LeadFlow

6. How long did it take you to set up LeadFlow?
7. What features do you use most?
8. How often do you use it? (daily, weekly, etc.)
9. What surprised you about using it?
10. Was anything confusing or hard to use?

### Part 3: Results

11. How many extra deals have you closed since using LeadFlow?
12. What's the total commission from those deals?
13. How much time does LeadFlow save you per week?
14. What's the best outcome you've had from using LeadFlow?
15. Would you recommend LeadFlow to other agents? Why?

### Part 4: Quote

16. If you had to tell another agent why they should use LeadFlow in one sentence, what would you say?

## Case Study Format (Long Version)

**Use this for blog posts, email sequences, and detailed marketing.**

# How David Went From Losing Leads to Closing 3 Extra Deals Per Month

**Agent:** David Mwangi  
**Location:** Kilimani, Nairobi  
**Specialty:** Residential apartments  
**Experience:** 5 years

## The Challenge

David is a successful real estate agent handling 50-60 property inquiries weekly via WhatsApp.

But success came with a problem: **information overload.**

"I'd get 10 inquiries before lunch," David explains. "By evening, I'd forget who asked about what property. I'd lose track of hot leads."

His system? Notebooks and WhatsApp stars.

"I tried writing names in a notebook. Starred important messages. But it was chaos. I'd forget to follow up with people who were ready to buy."

The breaking point came when David lost a KES 60,000 commission.

"A client asked about a 3-bedroom in Kilimani. I said I'd check and get back to them. Got busy. Forgot. They bought from another agent two weeks later."

That's when he decided something had to change.

## The Discovery

David saw a post in the "Real Estate Agents Kenya" Facebook group about LeadFlow Kenya.

"At first I thought, 'Another tool?' I'd tried CRMs before. Too complicated."

But the pitch was different: **A simple dashboard for WhatsApp leads. That's it.**

"I liked that it wasn't trying to be everything. Just solve one problem: keep track of WhatsApp leads."

He signed up for the free trial.

## The Transformation

Setup took 5 minutes.

"I connected my WhatsApp. Boom. All my leads were there in one list."

David started tagging leads:
- 🔥 Hot (ready to view/buy)
- ⚡ Warm (interested, need follow-up)
- ❄️ Cold (browsing, not urgent)

"Game changer. I could see at a glance who needed attention."

The feature he uses most? **Follow-up reminders.**

"I set a reminder: 'Call John about Westlands apartment tomorrow 2 PM.' LeadFlow sends me a WhatsApp notification. I never miss follow-ups now."

## The Results (First 30 Days)

**3 closed deals** that David says he would have missed before LeadFlow.

**Deal 1:** Client from 2 weeks prior he'd forgotten about. Reminder popped up. He called. They viewed. They bought.  
**Commission: KES 45,000**

**Deal 2:** Warm lead he'd tagged. Follow-up reminder nudged him to check in. They were ready to move forward.  
**Commission: KES 50,000**

**Deal 3:** Hot lead that moved faster than expected. Saw in dashboard they'd messaged 3 times in one day. Prioritized them.  
**Commission: KES 40,000**

**Total additional revenue: KES 135,000 in month one.**

**Time saved:** 2 hours daily.

"Before, I'd spend 30 minutes every morning scrolling through WhatsApp trying to remember who said what. Now I open LeadFlow, see everything organized, and start working."

## The Recommendation

Would David recommend LeadFlow to other agents?

"Absolutely. If you get more than 20 WhatsApp leads per week, you need this."

His advice to agents on the fence:

"Do the math. If it helps you close ONE extra deal in a year, you're up KES 45,000. LeadFlow costs KES 24,000 per year. It pays for itself."

**ROI: 87.5%**

"I'm not a tech person. If I can use it, anyone can."

### Filming Tips

**Do's:**
- ✅ Natural, conversational tone
- ✅ Show personality
- ✅ Mention specific numbers
- ✅ Film in one take (feels authentic)
- ✅ Look at camera (eye contact)

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Read from script (sounds fake)
- ❌ Overproduce (TikTok-style works best)
- ❌ Make it perfect (rough edges = authentic)

## Permission & Compensation

**Always get permission:**

"David, thank you for sharing your results! Would you be comfortable if I used this as a case study on our website and in our marketing? I'd include your name, photo, and what you shared here. Totally okay to say no!"

**If they say yes:**

"Awesome! Can I send you a short form to sign giving permission? Just protects both of us."

**Compensation options:**

1. **Free month** - "Next month on us as a thank you"
2. **Lifetime discount** - "50% off forever"
3. **Cash** - "KES 5,000 for your time" (if you have budget)
4. **Feature in marketing** - "We'll feature you on our homepage"

**Recommendation:** Free month is fair and most will say yes.

**Example:**

- No specifics
- No numbers
- No story
