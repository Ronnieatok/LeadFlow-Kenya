## Budget Recommendations

### Testing Phase (Month 1)
- Daily budget: KES 1,000 - 2,000
- Run all 4 ad sets simultaneously
- After 3 days, pause lowest performers
- Double budget on best performer

### Scaling Phase (Month 2+)
- Once you find winning ad: KES 3,000 - 5,000/day
- Expected cost per lead: KES 100 - 300
- Expected conversion rate: 2-5%

**Example:**
- Budget: KES 2,000/day × 30 days = KES 60,000/month
- Clicks: 200-300 @ KES 200-300 per click
- Signups: 10-15 @ KES 4,000-6,000 per signup
- Paid customers (30% conversion): 3-5 customers
- Revenue: 3 × KES 2,000 = KES 6,000/month
- Month 2 retention: KES 12,000
- Month 3: KES 18,000 (compounding!)

## Landing Page A/B Tests

Once ads are running, test these variations:

1. **Headline Test:**
   - Current: "Stop Losing Property Leads on WhatsApp"
   - Test: "Close 30% More Deals Without Working Harder"

2. **Pricing Display:**
   - Show pricing upfront vs hide until scroll
   
3. **CTA Button Text:**
   - "Get Early Access" vs "Start Free Trial" vs "Join 50+ Agents"

4. **Hero Image:**
   - Dashboard screenshot vs happy agent vs cluttered WhatsApp

Use Google Optimize (free) or run separate URLs for 7 days each.
