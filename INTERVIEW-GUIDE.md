# Customer Interview Guide

Use these questions to validate the idea and discover what features to build.

---

## When to Interview

**Pre-Launch Interviews** (Week 1):
- Goal: Validate the problem exists
- Who: 10 real estate agents
- Method: WhatsApp call or in-person coffee

**Beta Tester Interviews** (Week 2):
- Goal: Understand how they use the MVP
- Who: 3-5 beta testers
- Method: Screen share + observation

**Paying Customer Interviews** (Week 4+):
- Goal: Build what they actually need
- Who: Your first 5 customers
- Method: Monthly check-in calls

---

## Pre-Launch Interview (15 minutes)

**Goal:** Confirm they have the problem and would pay to solve it.

### Opening (1 minute)

"Hi [name], thanks for chatting with me. I'm building something for real estate agents and wanted to get your input. This isn't a sales call—just learning. Cool if I ask you some questions about how you manage WhatsApp leads?"

### Discovery Questions (10 minutes)

**1. Current Process**
- "Walk me through what happens when you get a property inquiry on WhatsApp."
- "How do you keep track of all your leads?"
- "What tools do you currently use?" (pen/paper, Excel, CRM, nothing?)

**2. Pain Points**
- "What's the most frustrating part of managing WhatsApp leads?"
- "Tell me about a time you lost a deal because of disorganization."
- "How many leads would you say you lose track of per week?"

**3. Impact**
- "How much commission do you make per deal on average?"
- "If you could close just 1-2 more deals per month, what would that mean for you?"

**4. Current Solutions**
- "Have you tried any tools to help with this?"
- "What didn't work about them?"

**5. Willingness to Pay**
- "If there was a tool that captured every WhatsApp lead, reminded you to follow up, and helped you close more deals, would that be valuable to you?"
- "What would you expect to pay for something like that per month?"
- "Would KES 2,000/month be worth it if it helped you close 1 extra deal?"

### Closing (2 minutes)

- "This is super helpful. I'm thinking of building this properly."
- "Would you be open to testing an early version for free?"
- "Can I add you to the beta tester list?"

### Key Insights to Listen For

✅ **Green flags** (build it!):
- "I lose at least 5 leads per week"
- "I've tried Excel but it's too manual"
- "I'd easily pay KES 2,000 if it helps me close more deals"
- "When can I try this?"

🚩 **Red flags** (don't build):
- "I only get 2-3 inquiries per week" (not enough volume)
- "I have a system that works fine" (no pain point)
- "That's too expensive" (won't pay)
- "Maybe..." (not committed)

## Beta Tester Interview (30 minutes)

**Goal:** Watch them use the product. Find bugs and missing features.

### Setup (2 minutes)

"Thanks for testing LeadFlow! I'm going to watch you use it and take notes. Don't worry about breaking anything—I need to see what's confusing or broken. Share your screen?"

### Observation (20 minutes)

**Don't talk. Just watch.**

Ask them to:
1. "Show me how you'd add a new lead from WhatsApp"
2. "Tag this lead as 'Hot'"
3. "Set a follow-up reminder for tomorrow"
4. "Show me your lead list and explain what you see"
5. "Send a message to one of your leads"

**Take notes on:**
- Where do they get stuck?
- What do they expect to happen vs what actually happens?
- What features do they look for but can't find?
- What's their facial expression? (confused, frustrated, happy?)

### Questions (8 minutes)

**1. First Impressions**
- "On a scale of 1-10, how easy was that to use?"
- "What was confusing?"
- "What felt natural?"

**2. Value**
- "Is this solving your problem?"
- "What's missing?"
- "What feature would make this a must-have for you?"

**3. Usage**
- "How often do you see yourself using this?" (daily, weekly, monthly)
- "What would make you use it more?"

**4. Pricing**
- "After trying it, does KES 2,000/month feel fair?"
- "Would you pay for this when the trial ends?"

### Red Flag Responses

If they say:
- "It's okay but I'd stick with Excel" → Not solving the problem well enough
- "I'd need [complex feature] before paying" → Scope creep, focus on core value
- "I forgot about it for the past 3 days" → Not solving a real pain point

## Paying Customer Interview (20 minutes monthly)

**Goal:** Keep them happy. Build what they need. Get testimonials.

### Check-in (5 minutes)

- "How's LeadFlow working for you?"
- "Are you using it daily?"
- "Any issues or bugs I should know about?"

### Usage Patterns (5 minutes)

- "What features do you use most?"
- "What features have you never used?"
- "Is there anything you wish it did but doesn't?"

### Outcomes (5 minutes)

- "Have you closed any deals you think you would have missed without LeadFlow?"
- "How much time does this save you per week?"
- "What's the best part about using it?"

**If they mention a win:**
- "That's amazing! Would you be comfortable sharing that as a testimonial?"
- "Can I use your name/photo or do you prefer to stay anonymous?"
- "Could we do a short video testimonial? Just 1 minute on your phone."

### Feature Requests (5 minutes)

- "If you could add one feature, what would it be?"
- "Why is that important to you?"
- "How would that change how you use LeadFlow?"

**Prioritization Question:**
- "Which would help you more: [Feature A] or [Feature B]?"

## Sample Interview Script (Full Example)

**You:** Hi David! Thanks for jumping on this call. I know you're busy so I'll keep this quick—about 15 minutes. I'm building something for real estate agents and wanted to get your input. This isn't a sales call, just learning. Sound good?

**Agent:** Yeah, sure.

**You:** Cool. So, walk me through what happens when you get a property inquiry on WhatsApp. Like, someone messages you asking about an apartment—what do you do?

**Agent:** Usually I reply right away with the basics—price, location, photos. Then I try to get their budget and schedule a viewing.

**You:** Got it. And how do you keep track of all these conversations? Do you use anything specific?

**Agent:** Honestly, just WhatsApp. Sometimes I'll star important messages or write names in my notebook.

**You:** Okay, so notebook and stars. Does that system work well for you?

**Agent:** Not really. (laughs) I forget to follow up all the time. Last week I had a hot lead I was supposed to call back and I completely forgot. They went with another agent.

**You:** Ouch. How often does that happen?

**Agent:** Probably 3-4 times a month I miss follow-ups. Sometimes more when I'm busy.

**You:** If each of those is a potential deal, and you make, what, KES 45K commission per deal...

**Agent:** Yeah, around there. Sometimes more.

**You:** So that's potentially KES 180K per month you're leaving on the table just from missed follow-ups?

**Agent:** (pauses) When you put it like that, yeah. That hurts to hear. (laughs)

**You:** Have you tried any tools to help with this? Like a CRM or something?

**Agent:** I tried a couple but they were too complicated. Too many fields to fill in. I just want something simple.

**You:** Makes sense. So here's what I'm thinking about building: A simple dashboard that shows all your WhatsApp leads in one place, lets you tag them as hot/warm/cold, and sends you reminders to follow up. That's it. Nothing fancy.

**Agent:** That would actually help.

**You:** If something like that helped you close even 1-2 extra deals per month, what would you be willing to pay for it?

**Agent:** I mean, if it's actually working and helping me close deals, KES 2,000, maybe 3,000 per month is nothing. That's less than one commission.

**You:** Awesome. Would you be open to testing an early version? Free for you, and I just need your honest feedback on what works and what doesn't.

**Agent:** Yeah, I'm down. When can I try it?

**You:** I'll add you to the list. Should be ready in about 2 weeks. I'll WhatsApp you. Thanks so much for your time!

## Post-Interview Actions

**After Each Interview:**
1. **Write notes immediately** (within 1 hour while fresh)
2. **Tag insights**:
   - Pain points mentioned
   - Features requested
   - Willingness to pay
   - Red flags
3. **Add to spreadsheet**:
   - Name
   - Contact
   - Key quote
   - Interest level (High/Medium/Low)
   - Follow-up date

**After 5 Interviews:**
- Look for patterns: What did 3+ people say?
- Identify the #1 pain point everyone shares
- Determine if there's a real problem worth solving
- Decide: Build or kill?

**After 10 Interviews:**
- You should have clear answers to:
  - What's the main problem?
  - What features do people need most?
  - What's the right price?
  - Who are your ideal customers?

- Use Otter.ai (free tier) to transcribe audio

## Red Flags That Mean "Don't Build"

Stop if you hear these patterns across interviews:

❌ "That's a nice-to-have but not essential"  
❌ "I don't get enough leads to need this"  
❌ "My current system works fine"  
❌ "Maybe in the future"  
❌ "Can you build [20 features] before I'd use it?"

**If 7/10 interviews are lukewarm, don't build it.**

---

## Green Flags That Mean "Keep Going"

Build immediately if you hear:

✅ "I need this yesterday"  
✅ "How much? I'll pay now"  
✅ "This is exactly my problem"  
✅ "When can I start using it?"  
✅ "I know 10 other agents who need this"

**If 7/10 interviews are enthusiastic, you have something.**

---

## Sample Questions Cheat Sheet

Keep these on your phone during interviews:

**Problem Validation:**
- Tell me about the last lead you lost
- What's the most frustrating part of your day?
- How many hours do you spend on [task] per week?

**Solution Validation:**
- Would [feature] solve that problem?
- Which is more important: [A] or [B]?
- What's missing from this?

**Pricing Validation:**
- What do you currently pay for [similar tool]?
- What would this be worth to you?
- Would you pay [price] for this?

**Commitment Validation:**
- When would you want to start using this?
- Can I sign you up for beta?
- Would you recommend this to other agents?

---

Remember: **Listen more than you talk.** The best interviews are 80% them talking, 20% you asking questions.
