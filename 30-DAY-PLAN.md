# 30-Day Validation & Launch Plan

This plan helps you validate the WhatsApp Lead Manager SaaS idea BEFORE building the full product.

**Goal:** Get 5 paying customers by Day 30

---

## Week 1: Landing Page + Pre-Validation

### Day 1-2: Deploy Landing Page
- [ ] Deploy to Vercel (use DEPLOY.md guide)
- [ ] Set up custom domain (optional but recommended)
- [ ] Test form submission on mobile and desktop
- [ ] Add Google Analytics
- [ ] Set up Supabase database for lead capture

### Day 3: Manual Validation
- [ ] Join 5 Facebook groups for real estate agents
- [ ] Post in groups: "Quick poll: What's your biggest pain point managing WhatsApp leads?"
- [ ] DM 10 active agents asking about their lead management process
- [ ] Record findings: Do they actually have this problem?

**Success Metric:** 7/10 agents confirm they struggle with WhatsApp lead management

### Day 4-5: Soft Launch
- [ ] Post in Facebook groups: "I built a tool to solve [problem]. Looking for 5 beta testers."
- [ ] Share landing page link
- [ ] Offer: "Free forever for first 5 testers in exchange for feedback"

**Success Metric:** 10+ landing page visitors, 3+ email signups

### Day 6-7: Manual MVP Test
- [ ] Reach out to 3 signups individually via WhatsApp
- [ ] Offer to manually manage their leads for 1 week using Google Sheets
- [ ] Track: What features do they actually use? What do they ask for?
- [ ] Key question: "Would you pay KES 2,000/month if this was automated?"

**Success Metric:** At least 1 agent says "yes, I'd pay for this"

---

## Week 2: Build Minimum Viable Product

### Day 8-10: Core Features Only
Don't build everything. Build ONLY:
- [ ] WhatsApp Business API integration (or Baileys)
- [ ] Simple lead inbox (list view of WhatsApp conversations)
- [ ] Basic tagging (Hot/Warm/Cold)
- [ ] M-Pesa payment integration (Daraja API)

**What NOT to build yet:**
- ❌ Advanced analytics
- ❌ Team collaboration
- ❌ Custom reports
- ❌ Mobile app

### Day 11-12: Test with Beta Users
- [ ] Give access to 3 beta testers from Week 1
- [ ] Schedule 30-min onboarding call with each
- [ ] Watch them use it (screen share)
- [ ] Note: Where do they get confused? What do they love?

### Day 13-14: Fix Critical Bugs
- [ ] Fix top 3 bugs from beta testing
- [ ] Add the ONE feature everyone asked for
- [ ] Prepare pricing page with M-Pesa payment

**Success Metric:** Beta testers use it daily for 3+ days straight

---

## Week 3: Paid Launch

### Day 15: Create Offer
- [ ] Pricing decision: KES 2,000, 3,000, or 4,500/month?
- [ ] Launch offer: "First 10 customers get 50% off lifetime"
- [ ] Set deadline: "Offer expires in 7 days"

### Day 16-18: Facebook Ads
- [ ] Create 4 ad sets (use MARKETING.md)
- [ ] Budget: KES 2,000/day (total KES 6,000 for 3 days)
- [ ] Target: Real estate agents in Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu
- [ ] Track: Cost per click, cost per signup, conversion rate

**Success Metric:** 50+ landing page visitors, 5+ email signups

### Day 19-21: Personal Outreach
- [ ] Message 20 agents in Facebook groups
- [ ] Script: "Hi [Name], I saw your post about [property]. I built a tool that helps agents manage WhatsApp leads. Free trial for 30 days. Interested?"
- [ ] Follow up with demo calls
- [ ] Close 2-3 paid customers

**Success Metric:** 2 paying customers @ KES 2,000/month = KES 4,000 MRR

---

## Week 4: Optimize & Scale

### Day 22-24: Customer Success
- [ ] Daily check-ins with paying customers
- [ ] Fix any blocking bugs immediately
- [ ] Ask: "What would make this a 10/10 tool for you?"
- [ ] Implement top requested feature

### Day 25-27: Case Study
- [ ] Interview your best customer
- [ ] Ask: "How many extra deals did you close? How much time did you save?"
- [ ] Write case study with numbers
- [ ] Get permission to use their name/photo

### Day 28-30: Double Down
- [ ] Post case study in Facebook groups
- [ ] Increase Facebook ad budget to KES 3,000/day
- [ ] Email sequence to landing page signups (use EMAIL-SEQUENCE.md)
- [ ] Close 3 more customers

**Success Metric:** 5 total paying customers @ KES 2,000 = KES 10,000 MRR

---

## Kill Criteria (When to Stop)

Stop building if:

❌ Week 1: Less than 3 agents say they'd pay for this  
❌ Week 2: Beta testers don't use it after Day 3  
❌ Week 3: Zero paying customers after ads + outreach  
❌ Week 4: Customers cancel within first week

**If you hit kill criteria:** Pivot or move to next idea. Don't waste months on something nobody wants.

---

## Success Criteria (When to Double Down)

Keep building if:

✅ 5+ paying customers by Day 30  
✅ Customers actively use it 4+ days/week  
✅ At least 1 customer says "I'd recommend this"  
✅ You see clear path to 20 customers in Month 2

**If you hit success criteria:** You have product-market fit. Now scale!

## Red Flags to Watch For

🚩 **Problem:** No one signs up for landing page  
**Fix:** Your messaging is wrong. A/B test headline.

🚩 **Problem:** People sign up but don't activate trial  
**Fix:** Email them asking why. Maybe onboarding is too hard.

🚩 **Problem:** Trial users don't convert to paid  
**Fix:** They don't see value. Add the feature they actually need.

🚩 **Problem:** Customers cancel after 1 month  
**Fix:** Product doesn't work or solve real problem. Interview them.

---

## When You're Stuck

**Problem:** Can't get first customer  
**Solution:** Lower price to KES 1,000 or offer lifetime deal for KES 20,000 upfront

**Problem:** Product is buggy  
**Solution:** Fix bugs before getting more customers. 1 happy customer > 10 frustrated ones

**Problem:** Running out of money  
**Solution:** Pause ads. Focus on organic (Facebook groups, WhatsApp, word of mouth)

**Problem:** Not sure what to build next  
**Solution:** Ask your customers. Build what they ask for most.

---

## Founder Mindset

Remember:

1. **Ship fast, fix later** - Bugs are okay if core value works
2. **Talk to users daily** - They'll tell you what to build
3. **Revenue = validation** - Free users don't count
4. **Focus on 10X ROI** - If it saves agents KES 20K, charge KES 2K
5. **Kill features, not customers** - Remove features no one uses
